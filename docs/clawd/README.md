# Clawd in the notch

Every animation Clawd plays in the notch, and when he plays it. Clawd is the mascot of Claude by Anthropic. Claude Notch doesn't ship his sprites: it reads them at launch from the Claude desktop app. Without the Claude app, a simpler hand-drawn Clawd types on a laptop instead. These GIFs come from the app's own animation code, so they show exactly what you get in the notch.

## Resting

After a task Clawd stays in the notch for 15 more minutes, so you can see that Claude is done. Every 30 seconds he does one of these, in no fixed order and never the same twice in a row: looks around, sways, dances, does the hula. In between he doesn't freeze: he breathes, blinks (now and then twice) and sometimes glances aside or up. So he does whenever he stands still, living in the notch for good too.

## Living in the notch for good

Switch on **Clawd stays in the notch** in the settings (Clawd's menu → Settings…) and he floats down into it under a parachute, waves, and never walks off: when Claude has nothing going on, he stays in the notch and keeps himself busy. Every few seconds he does something, in no fixed order, and nothing comes back until six other things have gone by. Nothing turns up out of thin air: he takes it out from behind his back and puts it back there when he's done.

- brings a mug of coffee out from behind his back: steam curls up, and now and then he takes a sip with his eyes shut in a smile;
- lifts headphones from behind his back over his head, puts them on and nods to the beat with his eyes shut, notes floating off;
- lifts a wizard's hat from behind his back over his head and puts it on, then brings out a wand and holds it up: sparkles fly off its star, and he winks;
- lifts a chef's hat from behind his back over his head and puts it on, then brings out a frying pan and tosses a pancake;
- stands thinking with a hand on his chin, dots coming and going in a bubble;
- looks around, sways, dances (once or twice), does the hula, hops twice, gets excited, breakdances (the whole routine, with a windmill and a headspin);
- sits down for a nap under an open book and gets back up;
- walks off out of the notch and comes back a few seconds later, walking or under a parachute.

The props are drawn in Claude Notch's code over Clawd's own frames from the Claude app. None of it is what he does at work or when Claude needs you, so it can't be taken for news. The checkmark and the time of the last task still go away after 15 minutes, and a new task still comes first.
