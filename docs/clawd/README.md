# Clawd in the notch

Every animation Clawd plays in the notch, and when. Clawd is the mascot of Claude by Anthropic. Claude Notch doesn't ship his sprites: it reads them from the Claude desktop app at launch (without it, a simpler hand-drawn Clawd stands in). These GIFs are drawn by the app's own animation code, so they're exactly what you see in the notch.

## Living in the notch for good

## Props
Nothing turns up out of thin air: a book, a mug, hats, a broom, a rocket — he takes it out from behind his back and puts it back there. And when a task comes in while he's busy with something, he puts it away first, then gets back to work.

The props are drawn in Claude Notch's code over Clawd's own frames from the Claude app.
